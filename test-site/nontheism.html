the god of nontheism is neither omnipotent, omnipresent, nor omnibenevolent. nobody believes in her, not even herself. as for powers, she can correctly distinguish between light and dark 50% of the time, in laboratory conditions.

if you pray to the god of nontheism, she will only answer to say "the number you have dialed is out of service." I stayed on the line once, anyway. she eventually continued, "you can't keep calling nobody."

"but you're somebody" I said
